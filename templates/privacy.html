Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run Spendly:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and request times.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers, or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • Store, display, and summarise your expenses.
  • Keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database on our servers. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser information when a page loads. Apart from this, we do not share your personal or expense data with third parties unless required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
